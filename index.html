MiBubbl Community Guidelines

MiBubbl is built as an intentional, adult (18+) space designed for authentic expression, genuine connections, and creative freedom. We believe adults should be trusted to curate their own feeds and express themselves without heavy-handed censorship.

However, freedom of expression works best when everyone respects basic boundaries. To keep MiBubbl safe, clean, and enjoyable, we maintain a few clear-cut lines on what stays and what goes.

1. What’s Welcome Here (The "Do's")

We want MiBubbl to be a vibrant canvas for your creativity and daily life. You are free to share:

  • Artistic & Creative Expression: Photography, styling, alternative fashion, and artistic body expression.
  • Lifestyle & Casual Content: Swimwear, bikinis, beachwear, fitness content, and everyday moments.
  • Open Conversation & Healthy Debate: Real thoughts, moments, snippets, and vibes. We actively encourage lively discussions and differing perspectives. Disagreeing and debating ideas is a natural part of a thriving, intelligent community.

As long as it's consensual, legal, and authentic to you, you have the creative freedom to share it.

2. The Hard Lines (The "Don'ts")

While we love free expression and robust debate, certain things threaten the safety and integrity of the platform. The following content is strictly prohibited and will be removed:

  • Personal Attacks & Bullying: While debating ideas is fully encouraged, discussions must never cross into personal abuse. There is a bright line between challenging a thought and attacking an individual. Targeted harassment, malicious name-calling, and persistent bullying have no place here.
  • Child Safety & Zero Tolerance: Because MiBubbl is an adult (18+) platform, the presence or exploitation of minors is strictly prohibited. Any content depicting, involving, or exploiting minors—including Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or any form of predatory grooming and contact—is an immediate, permanent zero-tolerance violation.
  • Explicit Pornography & Non-Consensual Media: While artistic expression and swimwear are fully welcome, unedited explicit pornographic media, graphic sexual content, and any non-consensual imagery are a hard no.
  • Spam & Automated Promotion: Flooding the public feed with automated scripts, bot-driven engagement, crypto/financial scam links, or relentless unsolicited self-promotion.
  • Copyright Infringement: Stealing and reuploading commercial media, paid intellectual property, or professional work that isn't yours without permission.

3. Our Transparency Commitment (No "Shadowbans")

We hate the industry-standard "black box" moderation where platforms secretly hide or delete your content without explanation. If an administrator removes a post or moment, you will always receive a direct notification explaining exactly why (linked to the specific guideline violated). You'll never be left guessing.

4. The Enforcement Path

Our goal is never to trip people up, but to keep the platform healthy:

  • First Step: Content removal with a clear reason notification and a direct link back to these guidelines.
  • Child Safety Violations: Accounts found sharing or engaging in child safety violations are permanently banned instantly with no warnings or second chances, and where legally required or appropriate, reported to relevant authorities.
  • Repeated Violations: Ignoring platform boundaries and repeatedly violating standard community guidelines will lead to temporary profile restrictions, followed by permanent account closure.
